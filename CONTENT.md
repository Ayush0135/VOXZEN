## ❓ FAQ Content

### How does the 7-day free trial work?
Start with full access to your chosen plan for 7 days completely free. No credit card required upfront. After the trial, you'll be automatically enrolled in the monthly subscription. You can cancel anytime during the trial with no charges.

### How is Voxzen AI better than YouTube Auto-Dub?
YouTube Auto-Dub uses basic text-to-speech technology that often sounds robotic and unnatural. Voxzen AI uses advanced AI voice models trained on professional voice actors, delivering studio-quality dubbing that maintains the emotion, tone, and energy of your original content. Our creator voices sound authentic and engaging, leading to better viewer retention and engagement.

### What happens after the free trial ends?
After your 7-day trial, you'll be automatically charged for your selected plan on a monthly basis. You'll receive an email reminder 2 days before the trial ends. You can cancel anytime before the trial ends to avoid charges, or upgrade/downgrade your plan as needed.

### Can I change my plan later?
Absolutely! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. When upgrading, you'll get immediate access to new features. When downgrading, changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle, and you keep your current plan benefits until then.

### What languages are supported?
We support 40+ languages including Spanish, Hindi, Portuguese, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, and many more. Each plan comes with a specific number of language slots, and you can add more languages through our add-on packs.

### How does auto-fetch work?
Once connected, Voxzen AI monitors your YouTube channel for new uploads. When you publish a video, our system automatically fetches it, processes the dubbing in your selected languages, and uploads the dubbed versions. You can control this with the auto-dub toggle feature available in Pro Creator and Agency plans.

### Is there a limit on video length?
- Free plan: up to 15 minutes
- Creator: up to 30 minutes
- Pro Creator: up to 60 minutes
- Agency: unlimited

For videos longer than these limits, contact our sales team for custom pricing.

### How do add-ons work with my plan?
Add-ons are purchased separately and billed monthly alongside your base plan. They can be added or removed at any time. For example, if you need lip-sync for just a few videos, you can pay per video (₹499 each), or if you need it regularly, the monthly unlimited option (₹2,999) is more cost-effective.

### What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, UPI, net banking, and digital wallets. All payments are processed securely through industry-standard payment gateways.

### Can I get a refund?
We offer a satisfaction guarantee. If you're not happy with the service within the first 7 days of your paid subscription, contact our support team for a full refund. After 7 days, refunds are evaluated on a case-by-case basis.
